**How I checked:** For every reference with a DOI, I pulled the Crossref record to confirm the details and look for retraction notices. For references without a DOI, I used web searches or my own knowledge of the literature. One gap: for references marked † below I didn't run a retraction lookup, so their retraction status is unconfirmed.
